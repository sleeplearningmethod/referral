How it works

The Sleep Learning Method™

This isn't sleep training. It isn't cry-it-out in disguise. It's a progressive, responsive approach built around your child's readiness, not a rigid timeline or a generic plan.

1

We build readiness first

Before your child ever goes into the cot to settle alone, we do the foundational work. This is the stage everyone else skips — and it's why this feels so different. Many families notice sleep beginning to improve during this stage alone, before anything changes structurally.

2

We follow your child's sleep learning style

Every child has a unique sleep learning style. We go with the grain of your child — honouring their temperament, development, and nervous system — rather than forcing them through a generic plan that doesn't fit.

3

We support you through every step

You understand the why, not just the what. That means you can hold progress through disruptions, adapt as your child grows, and apply what you've learned with future children. The skills last because you understand what's actually happening.
